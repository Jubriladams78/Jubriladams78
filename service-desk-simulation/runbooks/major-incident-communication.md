# Major Incident Communication Template

Send the first update within **15 minutes** of declaring a P1, then every 30 minutes, even when nothing has changed.

---

**Subject:** [P1 – INVESTIGATING] Users unable to sign in – Started 08:12 CT

**What's happening:** Some employees are being locked out of their accounts after entering their password.

**Who is affected:** Employees across multiple sites. Clinical systems remain available for users already signed in.

**What we're doing:** IT has identified a likely cause and is working on a fix.

**What you should do:** Please do **not** keep retrying your password, because each attempt can lock your account again. If you are locked out and need urgent access for patient care, call the Service Desk at ext. 4357 (HELP).

**Next update:** 08:45 CT

---

**Status labels:** INVESTIGATING → IDENTIFIED → MONITORING → RESOLVED

**Rules I follow:**
- Plain language. No "Kerberos" or "GPO" in user-facing messages.
- Say what users should *do*, not just what is broken.
- Never guess at an ETA. Give the time of the next update instead.
