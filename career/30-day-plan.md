# 30-Day Plan: First Help Desk / IT Support Job

## Read this first
- **No plan guarantees an offer in 30 days.** Hiring cycles alone often run 2 to 6 weeks. The realistic goal for 30 days is **5 or more interviews**, with an offer possible if things go well. Interviews come from volume plus direct contact with people, not from more certifications.
- **You don't need another certification.** You have A+, Network+, Security+, AZ-104, and more. For help desk work, that's more than enough. Stop studying for new exams for these 30 days.
- **Your list of certifications can work against you.** A help desk manager who sees "Azure Solutions Architect Expert" on someone with no IT job may think "paper certs" or "will leave in 3 months." Lead with A+, Network+, Security+, ITIL, and your healthcare background. Mention the architect certifications lower down.
- **Your strongest angle is healthcare IT.** Twelve years working under HIPAA, daily EHR use, and supporting non-technical staff under pressure is exactly what hospital service desks need. Target hospitals and health systems first.

## Target roles (in priority order)
1. Service Desk Analyst / Help Desk Technician at hospitals and health systems (Chicago area: Northwestern Medicine, Advocate Health, Rush, UChicago Medicine, Endeavor Health, Ascension, Lurie Children's)
2. Desktop Support Technician
3. IT Support at MSPs. They hire juniors the fastest, though the pay is lower.
4. Contract roles through IT staffing agencies (Robert Half, TEKsystems, Insight Global, Apex Systems). **This is often the fastest way into a Fortune 500 company.** Contract-to-hire is how many people get their first IT role.

## Week by week

### Week 1: Build and package
- [ ] Push this `service-desk-simulation` project and pin it on GitHub
- [ ] Record a **3-minute screen video**: unlock a locked account, find the lockout source with Event 4740, and run `Get-EndpointHealth.ps1`. Post it on LinkedIn. A video of you doing the work is better proof than a list of certifications.
- [ ] LinkedIn headline: `IT Support / Service Desk | A+ Net+ Sec+ | Active Directory, M365, Windows 11 | 12 Yrs Healthcare (HIPAA, EHR)`
- [ ] Turn on #OpenToWork for recruiters only (the setting hidden from your current employer is fine)
- [ ] Rewrite your resume as a 1-page help desk version (see `resume-bullets.md`)
- [ ] Register with 3 staffing agencies and **call** them. Don't just apply online.
- [ ] Publish **Post 1**

### Week 2: Volume
- [ ] 25 applications and 25 manager or recruiter messages
- [ ] Publish **Post 2** (a ticket walkthrough)
- [ ] Practice 20 interview questions out loud (see `interview-prep.md`)

### Week 3: Follow up and interviews
- [ ] Follow up on everything from Week 1 (one polite follow-up, then move on)
- [ ] Publish **Post 3** (the phishing response)
- [ ] Do a mock interview: record yourself answering "Walk me through troubleshooting a user with no internet"

### Week 4: Close
- [ ] Publish **Post 4** (lessons learned)
- [ ] Send a thank-you email within 24 hours after every interview
- [ ] Widen the search if needed: MSPs, school districts, local government IT, and contract roles
