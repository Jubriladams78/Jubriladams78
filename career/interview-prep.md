# Help Desk Interview Prep

## Skills checklist: be able to explain AND demonstrate each one
**Must have (Tier 1)**
- [ ] AD: reset a password, unlock an account, find the lockout source, add a user to a group, and explain why access goes through groups
- [ ] M365: Outlook profile issues, MFA re-registration, OneDrive sync, Teams audio/video
- [ ] Windows 11: Event Viewer, Task Manager, `sfc /scannow`, safe mode, printer drivers, mapped drives
- [ ] Networking: DHCP vs. static, APIPA, DNS, `ipconfig`/`ping`/`nslookup`/`tracert`
- [ ] Ticketing: priority = impact × urgency, writing clear notes, when to escalate
- [ ] Security: phishing response, identity verification, least privilege
- [ ] Remote support tools (Quick Assist, TeamViewer, or similar)

**Strong to have**
- [ ] Intune / Autopilot basics, SCCM awareness
- [ ] ServiceNow. Create a free Personal Developer Instance at developer.servicenow.com and log your simulation tickets there for screenshots.
- [ ] Basic PowerShell (you have it)

## Questions you will be asked
1. **"Walk me through troubleshooting a user who has no internet."** Use KB-002, bottom-up.
2. **"A user is locked out repeatedly. What do you do?"** Use KB-001. Mention Event 4740.
3. **"Someone calls claiming to be the CFO and demands a password reset right now."** Verify identity anyway, follow the process, and escalate politely.
4. **"How do you prioritize three tickets at once?"** Impact × urgency. Clinical impact goes first.
5. **"Tell me about a time you dealt with a frustrated person."** Use a healthcare story (STAR format).
6. **"What would you do if you didn't know the answer?"** Gather information, check the KB, escalate with good notes, and learn the fix afterward.
7. **"With all these certifications, why help desk?"** Have this answer ready. See below.
8. **"Why IT, after 12 years in healthcare?"**

## Answer to #7 (you WILL get this question)
> "Certifications taught me the concepts, but I want to learn how a real enterprise environment runs, and the service desk is where you see all of it: identity, endpoints, network, security, and the users. In healthcare I learned that the person in front of you matters most, and that's what a help desk is. I'm planning to grow here, not pass through."

## STAR story template
- **Situation:** Where and when, in one sentence
- **Task:** What you were responsible for
- **Action:** What *you* did, with 2 or 3 specifics
- **Result:** The outcome, with a number if possible
