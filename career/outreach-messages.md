# Outreach Messages

**Who to message:** search LinkedIn for `"Service Desk Manager" OR "IT Support Manager" OR "Help Desk Supervisor"` and filter by location = Chicago. Also message **IT recruiters** at each target hospital, and people who were service desk analysts 1 to 2 years ago (they refer people).

**Rule:** Ask for feedback or advice, not a job. The job conversation follows naturally.

---

## Connection request to an IT manager (under 300 characters)
> Hi [Name], I'm moving into IT support after 12 years in healthcare (HIPAA, EHR). I built a service desk simulation with 10 worked tickets in AD and PowerShell. I'd value your feedback as someone who leads a help desk team. Thanks!

## After they accept
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Here's the project: [GitHub link]. It's 10 tickets from triage to root cause, plus a few PowerShell tools.
>
> One question: when you hire Tier 1 analysts, what separates the people who succeed from the ones who don't? I'm applying for Service Desk roles at [Company] and want to focus on what matters.

## When they have an open role
> Hi [Name], I saw [Company] is hiring a Service Desk Analyst (req #[X]). I applied today. I'd bring 12 years of supporting clinical staff under HIPAA, plus hands-on AD, M365, and Windows troubleshooting from my lab work: [link]. If you're the right person to talk to, I'd welcome a short call. If not, could you point me to the hiring manager?

## Staffing agency recruiter
> Hi [Name], I'm looking for help desk / desktop support contract or contract-to-hire roles in Chicago. I hold A+, Net+, Sec+, and AZ-104, I have 12 years in healthcare, and I have a hands-on service desk portfolio: [link]. I can start within [X] weeks. Do you have 10 minutes this week?

## Follow-up (5 to 7 business days later, send once)
> Hi [Name], following up on my note from last week. No pressure at all. If there's someone else on your team I should talk to about service desk openings, I'd appreciate the pointer.
